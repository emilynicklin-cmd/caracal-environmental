ABOUT CARACAL

Technology for more resilient landscapes.

Caracal Environmental is a South African environmental technology company developing practical monitoring and early-warning solutions for climate and landscape risks.

We combine environmental science, technology and local partnerships to turn environmental data into information that can support earlier and more effective action.

OUR CURRENT FOCUS

Detecting wildfires earlier.

Wildfires can spread rapidly across landscapes, threatening ecosystems, farms, infrastructure and communities. The sooner a fire is identified, the sooner a response can begin.

Caracal Environmental is developing an early-warning system designed to support faster veld fire detection and provide actionable alerts to those responsible for protecting landscapes and communities.

01

Detect

Monitor landscapes for potential fire events.

02

Verify

Assess detected events to reduce unnecessary alerts.

03

Deliver timely information to the people who need it.

04

Respond

Support faster mobilisation and informed response.

PILOT PROJECT

Testing the concept in the Overberg.

Caracal Environmental is working towards demonstrating its early wildfire detection concept in the Overberg region of South Africa.

The pilot will provide an opportunity to test the technology under real-world conditions and refine the system alongside local fire-management stakeholders.
