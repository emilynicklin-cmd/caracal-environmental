THE CHALLENGE

Environmental risks move faster than conventional monitoring.

Wildfires, floods and other landscape risks can escalate rapidly. Existing monitoring systems may depend on visual detection, manual observation or delayed information.

Caracal is being designed to provide earlier, ground-level intelligence so that response can begin sooner.

THE CARACAL EARLY WARNING MESH

Ground-truth intelligence, from landscape to response.

Solar-powered field nodes monitor conditions across the landscape and communicate through a long-range sensor network. Data is brought together within an intelligence platform to identify potential threats and distribute alerts to the people responsible for responding.

01

Sense

Field sensors monitor environmental signals such as smoke, carbon monoxide, temperature, wind and other local conditions.

02

Connect

Long-range communication links distributed sensor nodes across farms, reserves, catchments and other remote landscapes.

03

Interpret

Multiple sensor signals can be combined to identify potential hazards and support more informed decision-making.

04

Alert

Actionable alerts are delivered rapidly to response teams, land managers and other relevant stakeholders.

APPLICATIONS

One network. Different landscapes. Different risks.

The Caracal system is modular. Different sensor combinations can be deployed depending on the landscape and the risks that need to be monitored.

01

Wildfire

Smoke, carbon monoxide, temperature, wind and thermal sensing can provide ground-level indications of ignition and developing fire conditions.

02

Flood & Catchment Monitoring

Water-level, rainfall and soil-moisture monitoring can support early warning and environmental intelligence in rivers, wetlands and flood-prone areas.

03

Landscape Security

Acoustic sensing can support applications such as intrusion or gunshot detection across reserves and other remote landscapes.

04

Environmental Intelligence

A shared sensor network creates opportunities to expand monitoring as environmental risks, management needs and available technologies evolve.
